# Privacy Policy for PPL Project

**Last Updated:** August 6, 2026

**PPL Project** ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application **PPL Project** (the "Application" / "App", package name: `com.dark.ppl`).

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this Privacy Policy, please do not access or use the Application.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Information You Provide to Us
* **User Input & Preferences:** Information you provide when interacting with the application, configuring settings, or submitting input within the features.
* **Media & Files:** Files or media (e.g. photos or documents) that you explicitly select and upload using the app's file/image picker.

### B. Automatically Collected Information
* **Device Information:** We may collect non-personally identifiable technical information about your mobile device, including device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, and network connection status.
* **App Usage & Diagnostic Data:** Crash logs, performance metrics, and connection diagnostics to ensure server connectivity and app stability.

---

## 2. Permissions Used & Justifications

To provide full functionality, the Application may request the following permissions on your device:

| Permission | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `android.permission.INTERNET` | Required for communicating with backend servers, real-time messaging, and data synchronization. |
| `android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE` | Used to check internet connectivity and adapt connection states accordingly. |
| `android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS` | Used to deliver alerts, incoming message notifications, and status updates to your device. |
| `READ_MEDIA_IMAGES` / Media Access | Used solely when you choose to attach or select an image/video within the app. |

*Note: All permissions are requested in accordance with standard Android security protocols and can be revoked at any time through your device settings.*

---

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected via the Application to:
1. Provide, maintain, and improve the core features and functionality of the Application.
2. Establish real-time communication channels and deliver push notifications.
3. Diagnose technical issues, monitor server uptime, and improve user experience.
4. Prevent fraudulent or unauthorized activities and ensure application security.

## 5. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational security measures (including encryption in transit using HTTPS/WSS protocols) to safeguard your data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

## 6. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy:
