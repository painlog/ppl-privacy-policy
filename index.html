PPL Project ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your information when you use our mobile application PPL Project (package name: com.dark.ppl).

2. Permissions Requested & Justification

Permission Purpose
android.permission.INTERNET Required for connecting to remote servers, data synchronization, and core functionality.
android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Used to monitor active network connections and handle offline/online states.
android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS Used to send important real-time alerts and messages.
READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / Storage Used solely when you explicitly select images or files inside the application.

4. Third-Party Services

The Application may use third-party libraries that collect information in accordance with their privacy terms:

  • Google Play Services: Google Privacy Policy

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security practices, including data encryption in transit (HTTPS/WSS), to protect your personal data from unauthorized access or disclosure.

6. Data Deletion & User Control

You can reset or clear all locally stored data at any time by navigating to your device's Settings > Apps > PPL Project > Storage > Clear Data. You can also revoke any previously granted permissions directly in your device settings.

Our app does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under 13 years of age.
