Account & Data Deletion Request

We respect your privacy and provide transparent options to delete your account, personal data, and associated records from PPL Project.

1. How to Request Account & Data Deletion

You can request full deletion of your account and all associated data through any of the following methods:

Option A: Request via Email (Fastest Method)

Send an email request to our support team:

  • Email: drbatstore@gmail.com
  • Subject: Account and Data Deletion Request - PPL Project
  • Content: Please include your username, registered device identifier, or phone number used within the app so we can locate your record.

Our team will process your deletion request within 7 business days and send a confirmation reply once completed.

Option B: Local Device Data Deletion

To immediately remove all local cache, login credentials, and stored media from your mobile device:

  1. Open your device Settings.
  2. Navigate to Apps & Notifications > PPL Project.
  3. Tap Storage & Cache.
  4. Select Clear Storage and Clear Cache.
  5. Uninstall the application.

2. Data That Will Be Permanently Deleted

Data Category Action Taken
Account Profile & Identifiers Permanently deleted from the active database.
Message & Activity Logs Completely erased from the server.
Uploaded Media & Files Permanently destroyed from cloud storage.
Device Tokens (FCM) Revoked and invalidated immediately.

3. Data Retention Period

Retention Policy

  • Personal & Account Data: Deleted permanently within 7 days of verified request.
  • System & Diagnostic Logs: Non-personally identifiable server connection logs are automatically purged on a rolling 30-day cycle for security and fraud prevention.

4. Partial Data Deletion

If you wish to delete only specific transaction records or chat history without deleting your entire account, you can perform this directly within the app using the in-app reset / hapus features or by contacting us.
